SimpletonOS - open source notice and written offer for source code

SimpletonOS is built from free and open source software. It is a fork of
ROCKNIX (https://rocknix.org), itself a fork of JELOS, and of the
LibreELEC build system; our thanks to all three projects. The ROCKNIX
name and artwork are not used by SimpletonOS: ROCKNIX's branding is
licensed for non-commercial use only, and this credit is the only place
the name appears.

The software in this image is covered by the licences of its components:
the Linux kernel, busybox, systemd and most of the system under the GNU
GPL version 2 or 3 (some parts under the LGPL), the Music Player Daemon
(MPD) under the GPL version 2 or later, LVGL and many libraries under the
MIT or BSD licences, the fonts under the SIL Open Font License, and the
ARM Mali user-space GPU driver under ARM's end user licence (all texts in
/usr/share/licenses on this system). SimpletonOS's own code - the player
interface (simpleton-ui), the cache tool, the card and DAC scripts and
the MPD patches - is released under the GNU GPL version 2.

Source code

The complete source of SimpletonOS, including the build system, every
patch and the configuration that produced this image, is published at

    https://github.com/IamRoberty/distribution   (branch: next)

The exact commit this image was built from is the BUILD_ID line of
/etc/os-release on the device (Settings > System shows the version).

Written offer (GPL v2 section 3(b), GPL v3 section 6(b)): for three years
from the date you received this product, Simpleton will provide, to any
third party, a complete machine-readable copy of the corresponding source
code for the GPL- and LGPL-licensed components of this image, on a
medium customarily used for software interchange, for a charge no more
than the cost of physically performing the distribution. Requests may be
made through the project page above (open an issue) or in writing to the
address on the product's documentation.
